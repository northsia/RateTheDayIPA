# RateTheDayIPA

**Rate Your Day. Track Your Mood. Visualize Your Life.**

RateTheDayIPA is a simple iOS application that lets you rate your day and track your daily experiences through a visual chart. Record how each day feels and watch your personal history evolve over time.

## Features

- **Daily Rating** — Give each day a personal score.
- **Visual Statistics** — Track your daily ratings through charts.
- **Progress Tracking** — Look back at your previous days and observe trends.
- **Minimal Interface** — A clean and straightforward experience.
